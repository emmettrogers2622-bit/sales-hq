How We Built It — Step by Step

From first idea to a fully operational sales intelligence platform in under 48 hours.

Phase 1 — Foundation

Dashboard + Quad Data Ingestion

Built the core dashboard with Quad's real statistics: 8B mail pieces/year, 3B+ data points, 2,100+ brands. Created the futuristic dark UI with Quad brand colors (Red, Cyan, Yellow). Ingested all MX solutions, industries, and Quad company data.

Phase 2 — Case Studies & Content

15 Verified Case Studies + Mail Format Encyclopedia

Researched and added 15 real Quad case studies with verified metrics (Stanley Steemer 541% ROI, InnovAge -61% CPA, etc.). Built a 22+ format encyclopedia with physical descriptions, best uses, and sales talk tracks. Added a 23-piece visual mail gallery.

Phase 3 — Sales Tools

Outreach Engine + Pitch Builder + Objection Crusher

Built the Outreach Engine with email and LinkedIn modes, 8 message types per platform. Created the Pitch Builder with industry-specific talk tracks. Added Objection Crusher with rebuttals for every common pushback. Built 8 ROI calculators tied to case studies.

Phase 4 — Intelligence

87 Accounts + AI Chatbot "Harry"

Loaded all 87 accounts from CRM with company research, priority scoring, DM likelihood, recommended products, and matched case studies. Built the "Harry" AI chatbot trained on everything — accounts, pipeline, case studies, formats, competitors, Quad history, and sales methodology. Named after founder Harry V. Quadracci.

Phase 5 — Advanced Features

War Room + Meeting Prep + News Monitoring

Added the Deal War Room with drag-and-drop Kanban board for pipeline management. Built the Meeting Prep Generator (one-click full prep doc for any account). Set up weekly automated news monitoring across all 87 accounts. Embedded Harry Quadracci's biography and "Ready, Fire, Aim" principles into the chatbot.
